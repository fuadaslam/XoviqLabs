1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how xoviqlabs ("we", "us", or "our") handles your information in connection with the MockNerve mobile application ("App").

MockNerve is an offline-first study platform and practice test engine. We provide the software framework and tools for candidates to organize their studies, practice mock tests, track error patterns, and review flashcards.

Platform Only — No Preloaded Content: MockNerve does not supply, publish, host, or sell questions, answers, exam papers, or official curriculum content. All question banks, syllabi, notes, and study data are created, imported, or managed entirely by you (the user).

2. Information We Collect & User-Supplied Content

A. User-Created & Imported Study Data

All questions, answer keys, syllabus outlines, flashcards, and notes that you enter or import into the App remain your exclusive property and are stored strictly on your local device in a private, sandboxed SQLite database. We do not transmit, inspect, index, or store your imported study materials on our servers.

B. Local Study Activity

As you practice with your imported questions, the App records local analytical data on-device:

  • Practice Metrics: Quiz attempts, answer choices, accuracy rates, and time taken per question.
  • Revision Data: Error vault entries, custom bookmarks, and study streak counts.
  • App Preferences: Selected active syllabus, theme choice (Dark/Light mode), and local timer configurations.

C. Information We Do NOT Collect

MockNerve operates with zero user profiling. We do not collect:

  • Personal identifying details (such as your name, email address, or phone number).
  • User account credentials or passwords.
  • Precise GPS location or background location data.
  • Device advertising identifiers (IDFA / GAID) for tracking or ad profiling.
  • Contacts, media libraries, photos, camera, or microphone data.

3. How We Use Information

Because all processing happens on your device, your data is used solely to execute the App's core features:

  • Shuffling and presenting tests from your user-imported question banks.
  • Calculating local performance statistics, accuracy scores, and speed analytics.
  • Organizing your personalized Error Vault and bookmarks for targeted revision.
  • Persisting your custom syllabus hierarchies and study preferences.

4. Local & Offline Storage & Permissions

MockNerve functions 100% offline. The database, test player engine, and analytics run entirely on-device without requiring an active network connection.

  • Document Picker / File Access: When you choose to import your custom question files or export local database backups, the App utilizes the operating system's standard file picker (Storage Access Framework). The App does not scan or access any other files on your device.
  • No Intrusive Permissions: MockNerve requests no runtime access to your camera, microphone, contacts, or location.

5. Optional Google Drive Cloud Backup

MockNerve offers an optional cloud backup and restore feature so you can safeguard your self-created study database against device loss or transfer.

  • Direct Device-to-Cloud Transfer: If enabled, the backup transfer occurs directly between your mobile device and Google Cloud servers using official Google OAuth APIs.
  • Restricted Scope: The App requests only the restricted drive.appdata scope (Application Data folder). MockNerve can only access its own backup file and cannot read, view, or modify any other files in your Google Drive.
  • No Middleman Servers: xoviqlabs never intercepts, views, or stores your Google Drive credentials or backup files.

6. Optional AI Integration (Gemini AI)

MockNerve includes an optional AI Tutor feature to assist you in understanding concepts and questions you have imported.

  • Bring Your Own Key (BYOK): You provide your own personal API key obtained from Google AI Studio.
  • Hardware-Encrypted Key Storage: Your API key is stored exclusively on your device using hardware-backed secure storage (Android KeyStore / iOS Keychain via Expo SecureStore). It is never sent to xoviqlabs.
  • Direct API Communication: When you request an explanation, the specific question prompt is sent directly from your device to Google’s Generative Language API.

7. Third-Party Services & Privacy Links

MockNerve does not include third-party advertising SDKs, behavioral analytics trackers, or data-broker libraries. If you choose to use optional third-party integrations, their privacy practices are governed by their respective policies:

  • Google Identity & Google Drive: Google Privacy Policy ↗
  • Google Gemini API: Google AI Studio & Gemini API Terms ↗

8. Data Retention & Deletion

Because all your questions, tests, and progress are stored locally on your device, you maintain complete control over your data at all times:

  • In-App Reset: You can clear test histories, reset error vaults, or remove imported question banks anytime inside the App.
  • Immediate Deletion on Uninstall: Uninstalling MockNerve or clearing application storage in your device settings permanently and immediately deletes all local databases, imported questions, API keys, and study progress.
  • Cloud Backup Management: You can delete any saved cloud backups directly from Google Drive or revoke app access through your Google Account Security Dashboard.

9. Security

We employ industry-standard on-device security practices:

  • Sensitive credentials (such as optional user API keys) are stored in secure, hardware-encrypted device storage.
  • Local SQLite databases reside strictly within the operating system's sandboxed application storage.
  • All external communication (e.g., Google OAuth or Gemini API) is conducted over standard HTTPS/TLS encryption.

10. Children's Privacy

MockNerve is a study platform intended for general audiences and adult learners preparing for competitive and professional examinations. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13.
