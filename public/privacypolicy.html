Privacy Policy.

Last Updated: January 1, 2026

XoviqLabs Technologies ("XoviqLabs", "we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy and security. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, process, safeguard, and disclose information when you visit our website or engage our engineering services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information you directly provide to us, including:

  • Contact details such as name, email address, and phone number submitted via inquiry forms
  • Project scopes, system specifications, and architectural requirements
  • Technical metadata including IP address, browser type, device information, and analytics data collected to enhance website performance

2. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is utilized exclusively to:

  • Evaluate, scope, and deliver custom engineering and design solutions
  • Communicate sprint updates, staging checkpoints, and technical deliverables
  • Maintain security and prevent malicious activity across our infrastructure
  • Analyze website performance metrics to improve visitor experience

3. Data Confidentiality & Security

We never sell or monetize personal or client data. We utilize industry-standard encryption, access controls, and zero-trust cloud architectures to prevent unauthorized access. Confidential project information is protected under mutual non-disclosure agreements.

4. Third-Party Services

We work with trusted cloud and infrastructure providers (e.g. AWS, Vercel, EmailJS) that adhere to rigorous data privacy standards to operate our digital systems.

5. Contact & Data Rights

To request data modification, export, or deletion, please reach out directly to our privacy officer at info@xoviqlabs.com.
