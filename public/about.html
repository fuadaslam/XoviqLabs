Our Story

About XoviqLabs.

A modern development studio building AI-powered web and mobile applications from the ground up.

Who We Are

XoviqLabs is a newly founded software development studio. We started XoviqLabs with a clear mission: to make it simple and accessible for founders, entrepreneurs, and businesses to build modern web and mobile apps powered by the latest AI technologies.

We are a hands-on, passionate team of developers and designers. Because we are starting out, we put our absolute best effort into every client project—delivering clean code, responsive design, and smart AI features that truly work.

What We Do

Our core focus is simple and specialized:

  • Cross-Platform Mobile Apps: iOS and Android apps built with Flutter, designed for speed and smooth performance.
  • Modern Web Applications: Full-stack web apps, SaaS dashboards, and landing platforms built with Next.js and React.
  • Custom AI Integration: Adding smart AI chatbots, automated workflows, document analyzers, and OpenAI/Gemini features directly into your product.
